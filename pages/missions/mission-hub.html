How to Play

⌨ Typing Japanese

You don't need a Japanese keyboard — enable romaji input in your OS, then type normally and it converts automatically.

  • Windows: Settings → Time & Language → Language & region → Add Japanese. Switch with Win+Space.
  • Mac: System Settings → Keyboard → Input Sources → add Japanese (Romaji). Switch with Control+Space.
  • Phone/Tablet: Add the Japanese keyboard in your keyboard settings, switch via the globe icon.

📖 Study Room

Pick a lesson from the dropdown in the header. Read the grammar and vocabulary on the left, then type your answer into the practice box on the right.

🏯 Adventure Room

Move with WASD or the arrow keys — hold Shift to sprint. You can also just click anywhere on the floor to auto-walk there. Press E, Enter, or Space near a shelf or piece of furniture to interact with it (or just click it directly).
