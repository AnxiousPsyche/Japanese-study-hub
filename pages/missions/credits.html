💕 Credits

Neko Bunko is a lovingly hand-built side project by AnxiousPsyche — a fictional retro-OS library where studying JLPT N5 Japanese doubles as an excuse to hang out with pixel cats. None of it would exist without the open data, tools, and artwork below, so here's a proper thank-you, in the same spirit as the credits page on jisho.org (which is itself built on a lot of this same data).

Stroke-order data

Every hiragana and katakana stroke-order diagram in Kana Dojo is drawn from real stroke data published by KanjiVG — the same dataset jisho.org's own kanji/kana diagrams are rendered from. Copyright © 2009–2012 Ulrich Apel and contributors, licensed Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike 3.0.

Game engine

The Adventure Room's walkable library is built with Phaser 3, a free, open-source HTML5 game framework, licensed MIT.

Artwork & tilesets

The Adventure Room's interior tileset (floors, walls, doors, and furniture states) is the Top-down Retro Interior House pixel-art pack by Penzilla Design — free for non-commercial use with attribution; a commercial license applies for paid use.

Additional shelf, furniture, and prop art come from a handful of other free pixel-art packs collected while building the library scene — full attribution for those is still being tracked down and will be added here as it's confirmed.

The default player avatar photo is Sméagol, the real-life cat of this project's creator.

Fonts

Press Start 2P, Nunito, DotGothic16, and Space Mono are loaded from Google Fonts, each released under the SIL Open Font License by their respective designers. VT323 (used for the pixel-terminal display text throughout the Study Room and Kana Dojo) is self-hosted, designed by Peter Hull and also released under the SIL Open Font License.

Everything else

Lesson content, grammar explanations, example sentences, and the retro-OS shell itself are original work written for this project. JLPT N5 scope and ordering were referenced against standard public JLPT study guides, not any single copyrighted source.

Thanks for studying here. 🐾
